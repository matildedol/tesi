\emph{This thesis was written during my stay in Genova, at the Machine Learning Genoa Center (MaLGa) from July to November 2026, under the scientific supervision of Prof. Lorenzo Rosasco and with the guidance of Pietro Zerbetto. In light of my interest in the mathematical foundations of machine learning, Prof. Rosasco proposed studying classical generalization bounds and evaluating empirically how well they capture the qualitative behavior of the generalization gap. This idea originated the present work, a story of theory and evidence.}
