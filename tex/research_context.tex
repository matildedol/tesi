\emph{This thesis was written during my stay at the Machine Learning Genoa Center (MaLGa) in Genova from July to November 2026, under the scientific supervision of Prof.\ Lorenzo Rosasco. Building on my interest in the mathematical foundations of machine learning, Prof.\ Rosasco proposed studying classical generalization bounds and empirically assessing how well they capture the qualitative behavior of the generalization gap. His initial proposal and scientific guidance shaped the present work.}
